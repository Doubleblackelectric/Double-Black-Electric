EV Charger Installation in Whistler

Level 2 Home & Commercial Charging — Sea-to-Sky

Charge At Home, Wherever Home Is

Double Black Electric Inc. installs Level 2 EV chargers for homeowners, stratas, and businesses throughout Whistler, Pemberton, Squamish, and the rest of the Sea-to-Sky. Whether you're plugging in a Tesla, a Rivian, an F-150 Lightning, or any other EV, we make sure your charger is sized properly, installed safely, and inspected to code.

With over 18 years of experience and Red Seal certified Journeymen on staff, we handle everything from the panel review to the final commissioning — so you can pull into your driveway and just plug in. If your panel can't support the added load, we'll walk you through a panel upgrade first so the new charger has the headroom it needs.

  • Level 2 Charger Installation — Tesla Wall Connector, ChargePoint, Wallbox, FLO, JuiceBox, and any 240V hardwired unit
  • Load Calculations & Panel Reviews — confirm your existing service can handle the new load, or recommend an upgrade
  • Service & Subpanel Upgrades — 100A, 200A and 400A panel upgrades when your panel is full or undersized
  • Outdoor & Garage Wiring — weather-rated conduit, NEMA 14-50 outlets, and trenching for detached garages
  • Strata & Multi-Unit Installations — coordinated with strata councils and load management systems
  • BC Hydro Rebate Eligible Work — installations that qualify under current Go Electric and CleanBC programs

Done Right The First Time

Pemberton and Whistler winters are hard on electrical equipment — water, snow load, and cold-cycle stress all matter. We use proper outdoor-rated hardware, oversized conductors where needed, and dedicated breakers, so your charger runs reliably for the long haul.

  • Permitted & Inspected — every install is pulled, wired, and signed off through Technical Safety BC
  • Clean Routing — conduit runs that look intentional, not improvised
  • Right-Sized Breakers — sized for continuous EV load (80% rule), no nuisance trips
  • Future-Proof Conduit — pulled with extra capacity for second chargers or panel upgrades down the line
  • Honest Quoting — fixed-price where possible, hourly when scope is unclear, never both
