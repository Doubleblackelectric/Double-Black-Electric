Our Services

Professional electrical services throughout the Sea-to-Sky.

Residential

Whether you're building your dream home, upgrading during a renovation, or need quick support for an electrical issue, we provide professional results tailored to your needs.

  • New Builds — full electrical design and installation for custom homes
  • Renovations & Upgrades — rewiring, panel changes, lighting upgrades, and modern smart-home solutions
  • Service Calls & Repairs — troubleshooting, breaker replacements, outlet/switch repair, and safety inspections
  • Lighting Installation — pot lights, feature lighting, exterior/landscape lighting
  • Heating & Comfort Systems — baseboard heaters, in-floor heating, hot tub wiring
  • Safety Systems — smoke/CO detectors, surge protection, GFCI/AFCI installation
  • EV Chargers & Solar Integration — energy-efficient options for today's homeowner

Design

Our design services ensure your system is not only safe and functional but also tailored to match your lifestyle, aesthetics, and future needs. From modern lighting layouts to custom outlet and switch placement, we'll help you create a home that delivers your vision.

  • Custom Lighting Layouts — pot lights, feature lighting, and ambient solutions
  • Outlet & Switch Placement — designed around how you actually live and use your space
  • Smart Home Integration — seamless control of lighting, climate, and security
  • Energy Efficiency Planning — solar readiness, EV charging, and efficient circuit design
  • Renovation & Remodel Design — upgrading old layouts to meet today's standards

Commercial

Your business depends on reliable power. From new retail shop installations to ongoing electrical maintenance and repairs, we provide efficient, code-compliant solutions that keep your operations running smoothly.

  • Retail & Office Fit-Outs — complete wiring, lighting, and panel installations for new spaces
  • Repairs & Troubleshooting — fast response to electrical issues that impact your business
  • Lighting Upgrades — energy-efficient LED retrofits, display lighting, and exterior signage
  • Service & Maintenance — scheduled inspections, panel servicing, and safety upgrades
  • Equipment & Power Solutions — dedicated circuits for appliances, HVAC, and specialized machinery
  • Emergency Service Calls — quick, reliable support to minimize downtime

Custom Additions

From energy-efficient EV charging stations to luxury upgrades like saunas and hot tubs, we deliver safe, reliable installations that fit seamlessly into your lifestyle.

  • EV Charger Installations — fast, efficient charging solutions for electric vehicles
  • Hot Tub Wiring — safe, code-compliant installations for indoor and outdoor spas
  • Saunas & Specialty Equipment — custom wiring for high-demand systems
  • Outdoor & Landscape Power — dedicated circuits for exterior lighting and entertainment
