Reliable Power For Your Business

From a Whistler Village retail shop to a Pemberton restaurant or a Squamish workshop, your business depends on power that just works. Double Black Electric Inc. handles commercial electrical work across the Sea-to-Sky — from full retail fit-outs and tenant improvements to ongoing maintenance and emergency service calls.

We work cleanly, communicate clearly, and keep disruption to your operations minimal. And we pull every permit and book every inspection, so the work passes the first time.

  • Retail & Office Fit-Outs — complete wiring, lighting, and panel installations for new spaces and tenant improvements
  • Restaurant & Café Wiring — dedicated kitchen circuits, hood-fan controls, point-of-sale wiring, exterior signage
  • Lighting Upgrades & LED Retrofits — measurable energy savings, better light quality, longer lifespan
  • Service & Maintenance — scheduled inspections, panel servicing, and preventive maintenance; we also handle full panel upgrades when a space outgrows its existing service
  • Equipment & Power Solutions — dedicated circuits for HVAC, cooler/freezer banks, ovens, espresso, ski-tuning gear, machinery
  • Emergency Service Calls & Repairs — fast response when something fails and your shop is losing money by the minute
  • Code & Safety Upgrades — bringing older spaces up to current standards for insurance and licensing

Built For The Sea-to-Sky Business Reality

Our commercial work is shaped by the businesses we serve — seasonal swings, tight timelines, and customers who can't be inconvenienced by a half-finished install.

  • Off-Hours Scheduling — overnight and early-morning work to keep your storefront open during peak season
  • Coordination With GCs & Designers — we work cleanly alongside other trades on tenant-improvement projects
  • Documented Work — labeled panels, as-built notes, photos of buried runs — so the next electrician isn't guessing
  • Permits & Inspections Handled — Technical Safety BC permits pulled and signed off as part of every job that needs one
  • Insured & Bonded — fully covered for the kind of work commercial landlords expect to see
