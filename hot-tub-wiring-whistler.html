Hot Tubs Wired Right For Mountain Conditions

A hot tub is one of the best things you can have in the Sea-to-Sky — but only if it's wired safely. Double Black Electric Inc. handles full hot tub installations across Whistler, Pemberton, Squamish, and the rest of the Sea-to-Sky corridor. We pull permits, run weather-rated wiring, install the GFCI disconnect at the proper distance, and have the work inspected so it's done by the book.

From simple plug-and-play 120V swap spas to full 240V/60A custom installs with trenching and a new subpanel, we can handle the whole job — start to finish.

  • 240V / 50A & 60A Hot Tub Circuits — sized to manufacturer spec for any major brand (Arctic, Hydropool, Jacuzzi, Sundance, Bullfrog, Beachcomber)
  • GFCI Disconnect Installation — code-required disconnect, mounted at the right line-of-sight distance from the tub
  • Trenching & Direct-Burial Conduit — clean underground runs from the panel to the pad
  • Subpanel Installations — when a hot tub plus an EV charger plus a sauna add up to more than your existing panel can handle and you need a panel upgrade
  • Permits & Inspections — every install is pulled and signed off through Technical Safety BC
  • Indoor & Swim Spa Installations — bonded and grounded properly for indoor pools and swim spas

What A Proper Hot Tub Install Looks Like

We're not going to staple Romex along your fence and call it a day. A hot tub is wet, constantly powered, and used barefoot — it deserves the right materials and the right method.

  • Manufacturer Spec Sheet First — every tub has different requirements; we wire to the make and model, not a generic guess
  • Outdoor-Rated Hardware — NEMA 3R disconnect boxes, weatherproof connections, UV-stable conduit
  • Bonding — proper bonding of metal components to prevent stray current
  • Snow & Frost Considerations — buried at proper depth, conduit routed away from snow-load zones
  • Future Capacity — pulled with room to add lighting, audio, or a sauna circuit later
