Service Calls & Troubleshooting — Sea-to-Sky

Diagnose The Real Problem, Fix It Once

Electrical issues rarely fix themselves. A tripping breaker, a dead outlet, or a flickering light is your home telling you something — and ignoring it isn't a long-term plan. Double Black Electric Inc. responds to repair calls across Whistler, Pemberton, and Squamish — and the rest of the Sea-to-Sky corridor — diagnoses the actual root cause, and fixes the problem properly the first time.

We don't guess. We test, isolate, and resolve — then walk you through what we found and what we did about it.

  • Tripping Breakers & Blown Fuses — short circuits, overloaded circuits, faulty breakers; sometimes a sign your service is at capacity and you need a panel upgrade
  • Dead Outlets & Switches — loose connections, failed devices, miswired multi-way switches
  • Flickering & Buzzing Lights — bad dimmers, loose neutrals, voltage drop, failing fixtures
  • Burning Smells & Hot Devices — stop using the circuit and call us; this is genuinely urgent
  • GFCI & AFCI Faults — kitchens, bathrooms, garages, and exterior circuits like hot tub wiring that won't reset
  • Aluminum Wiring Issues — pigtailing and remediation in older Sea-to-Sky homes
  • Storm & Surge Damage — repairs after lightning or a power surge takes out devices

Honest Diagnosis, Honest Pricing

With over 18 years of experience, we know that the cheapest fix for the customer is usually the correct fix done once. We give you a straight answer about what's wrong, what's optional, and what's safety-critical.

  • Quick Response Times — we keep our schedule flexible enough to handle real service calls
  • Proper Testing Equipment — multimeters, circuit tracers, thermal imaging when needed
  • Clear Explanation — you'll understand what was wrong and what we did before we leave
  • Code-Compliant Fixes — no patchwork that creates a problem for whoever opens that wall next
  • Inspection-Ready Work — even on a small repair, we leave it the way we'd want to find it
