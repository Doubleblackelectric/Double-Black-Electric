Your Local Residential Electrician

Double Black Electric Inc. is a Pemberton-based residential electrician serving homes throughout Whistler, Pemberton, and Squamish — and the broader Sea-to-Sky corridor. Whether you're building a custom home, renovating a kitchen, or just need a ceiling fan installed cleanly, we bring the same professional approach to every job — small or large.

Owner James Pearse is a Red Seal certified Journeyman with over 18 years of experience. We're fully bonded, registered, and inspected — and we treat your home like we'd want ours to be treated.

  • New Custom Home Wiring — full electrical design and installation, from rough-in to final trim
  • Renovations & Additions — rewiring, lighting redesign, and panel/service upgrades to match a new layout
  • Suite & Carriage House Wiring — separate metering, dedicated panels, code-compliant secondary suites
  • Lighting Installation & Upgrades — pot lights, feature lighting, exterior & landscape lighting
  • Smart Home Integration — Lutron, Leviton, Kasa, and integrated lighting/climate control
  • Heating & Comfort Systems — baseboards, in-floor heat, mini-split disconnects, heat-pump wiring, and hot tub wiring
  • Service Calls & Repairs — quick response for breakers, outlets, switches, and lighting issues

Why Homeowners Choose Us

We're a small, owner-operated business. That means you talk to the same person who quoted the job, did the work, and is going to stand behind it. No revolving crews, no escalation chains.

  • Local & Reliable — based in Pemberton, so we're not driving up from the city for every call
  • Clean Workmanship — straight conduit runs, properly labeled panels, tidy job sites
  • Permitted & Inspected — every job that needs a permit gets one, full stop
  • Honest Quoting — fixed-price for defined scopes, hourly for unknowns, never confusing the two
  • Mountain-Tough Materials — we spec gear that holds up to Sea-to-Sky conditions
